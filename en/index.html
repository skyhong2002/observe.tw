tag.observe.tw

News Tags

One story, every outlet’s headline

Collects public articles from Taiwanese and selected international news sources, organizing tags, events, issues, outlets and bylines into data you can compare: rising keywords, each outlet’s headline for the same story, and text similarity and explicit citations between reports.

  • Headlines and event timelines — compare headlines and follow how related events develop
  • Media relationships and journalists — compare text similarity, shared bylines and explicit citations, with article evidence
  • Following, open API and live board — follow tags, outlets, journalists and events, with personal RSS, a public API and a rotating live board

youtube.observe.tw

YouTube Board

One story, every channel’s take

A dashboard of over three thousand public YouTube channels and more than a million videos: what news, TV and political channels are streaming right now, which issues are heating up, and how each channel covers the same story, by matching video titles against News Tags.

  • Live now and a video wall — current livestream rankings, concurrent viewers and many channels side by side
  • Trending tags and word cloud — news tags from tag.observe.tw matched against video titles and descriptions, ranked by channel-weighted heat
  • Issues and event timelines — AI-organized daily issues and how events unfold, with the cited videos to check against
  • Election reports — candidates and local political keywords by city

weblog.tw

Blog Watch

Every kind of life, each written its own way

A sister site to tag.observe.tw that turns the same methods on independent blogs: it collects articles from public RSS, Atom and sitemaps, then finds where blogs quote one another in their posts and which topics many of them are writing about right now.

  • Citations and a link map — sentences from the last 90 days that mention another blog, drawn into one site-wide map
  • Shared topics — words several blogs have taken up in the last 30 days, ranked by heat and how many blogs join in
  • Follow and personal RSS — sign in to follow blogs, then export your own RSS feed and OPML
  • Finds new blogs — weekly discovery; a candidate is added only once its feed and homepage check out

rep0rter.observe.tw

rep0rter

An AI reporter for the civic-tech community

Every hour it reads the public workspaces of civic-tech communities such as g0v, Code for Korea and Code for Japan — Slack, GitHub, Mastodon, RSS and public Notion — picks out the progress worth knowing about, and writes it up as short news stories.

  • Four languages — 繁體中文, English, 日本語 and 한국어, each story with cards linking its sources
  • Editorial rules — scoring and de-duplication; bots, CI noise and people who opt out are left out
  • Published everywhere — the website, RSS, a Telegram channel and Threads

site.observe.tw

Site Radar

Monthly traffic rankings and site tech for Taiwanese websites

Brings together GeneHong’s “Similar Web” spreadsheet and website traffic data: monthly Similarweb visit estimates for Taiwanese websites, organic-search and AI referral shares, and popularity bands from Cloudflare Radar and the Chrome UX Report. News Tags and Blog Watch read their traffic figures from here too.

  • Traffic rankings — filter by tag and source, compare any two months, download CSV
  • Tag overview — tag totals and rank trajectories across months, with open voting on site tags
  • Site tech — detects what each homepage is built with, using Wappalyzer rules

myzilla.observe.tw

MyZilla

My personal portal · browsing in review

A remake of MyZilla, the 2000s personal portal — bookmarks, clippings, multi-site search, movies and moods — plus a full review of your browsing history. The raw history stays on my own server and is never published.

  • Collections and insights — organize interests, select links with recommendations and publish revocable sharing snapshots
  • Accounts and friends — invite-only private accounts; interests are compared only when both sides agree

harmonica.observe.tw

Harmonica Observatory

Harmonica events, players and sheet music from around the world

A directory of the harmonica world that started in Taiwan and grew global: events, student clubs, ensembles, players, teaching studios, contest pieces and sheet-music sources. Public posts scattered across platforms and languages are gathered in one place, with the original text and links kept intact. The interface speaks 繁體中文, English, 日本語 and 한국어.

  • 24-hour Stories wall — Instagram Stories from the harmonica scene
  • Public event calendar — events worldwide and online in their original time zones, with Google Calendar and ICS feeds
  • Source directory and score index — filter by country and region; contest pieces by school year and ensemble type
  • Open submissions and data API — add or correct sources, plus an RSS feed and public API

localplaud

A self-hosted pipeline for Plaud recordings

Keep using the Plaud recorder and its official upload, but run transcription, speaker diarization, summaries, search and Q&A on my own machines — the raw audio comes back through Plaud’s Open API, and everything derived from it stays with me.

  • The full pipeline — transcode, transcribe, align, diarize and correct, then notes, chapters and mind maps
  • Ask across recordings — question one recording or the whole library, with playable citations
  • Runs on my hardware — deployed on the lab’s GPU host, with swappable local ASR and models
